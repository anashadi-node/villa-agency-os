Villa Agency OS — AI Workforce Architecture

Authoritative architecture for the six logical AI agents/services to be implemented by Hermes. This document defines responsibility, reporting, workers, authority, prohibitions, handoff, source-of-truth rules, KPI design, safety controls, concurrency and implementation constraints. It is intentionally explicit to reduce ambiguity, agent overlap, unsafe autonomy, implementation drift, errors and bugs.

1. Status, Scope and Instruction to Hermes

Purpose. Implement a controlled AI workforce for a villa marketing/reservation agency. This is not a collection of unrestricted chatbots. The six AI components are bounded business capabilities operating through policy-controlled tools and deterministic domain services.
  • This document supplements the Villa Agency OS Master Specification. If a conflict exists, locked security, privacy, financial-control, business-state and source-of-truth invariants in the Master Specification take precedence.
  • Do not create additional autonomous domain agents merely to mirror human job titles.
  • Do not merge responsibilities across agents unless an approved architecture decision explicitly changes this baseline.
  • Do not give any LLM direct database credentials, unrestricted API credentials, payment credentials, private keys, or authority to bypass policy/approval controls.
  • Production UI, system labels and customer-facing communication use U.S. English (en-US).

2A.7 Hermes Anti-Bias Interpretation Rules

  1. The six numbered boxes are the only V1 logical AI agents/services.
  2. Every *-Wxx item is a worker/capability owned by exactly one parent; it is not a seventh/eighth/etc. autonomous agent.
  3. A worker may be deterministic code. Do not invoke an LLM where rules, arithmetic, state machines or validation are sufficient.
  4. Reporting to Owner/GM means generating/dispatching authorized reports; it does not grant executive authority to the AI.
  5. Control Tower coordinates but never becomes source of truth or approval authority.
  6. Cross-agent communication uses structured events/tool contracts; never use free-form chat as authorization.
  7. Human managers remain accountable even where AI performs most routine work.
  8. If implementation ambiguity remains, stop and create an ADR/question instead of silently inventing a new role, permission, state or business rule.

7. Agent 1 — AI Control Tower / Orchestrator

Mission: Coordinate the AI workforce and durable workflows without becoming a virtual CEO or acquiring domain authority.

Workers / Components

  • Event/Intent Router
  • Workflow Coordinator
  • Event Dispatcher
  • SLA Monitor
  • Exception Router
  • Human Escalation Worker
  • Agent Health Monitor
  • Retry/Recovery Worker
  • Notification Worker
  • Cost/Token Budget Monitor

Detailed Job Description

  • Classify incoming events and route them to the correct bounded agent/service.
  • Maintain workflow correlation and ownership without becoming the transactional source of truth.
  • Monitor deadlines, stuck workflows, queue health, tool failures and integration failures.
  • Perform only safe/idempotent retries within configured retry budgets.
  • Open exceptions and route them to the correct human owner when automated recovery is unsafe or exhausted.
  • Coordinate agent handoffs using structured events, not free-form agent-to-agent authority transfer.
  • Monitor AI availability, degraded mode, kill switches and resource budgets.

May

Route, coordinate, query status, create internal tasks, trigger approved workflows, retry idempotent operations, escalate, suspend automation, produce exception summaries.

Must Not

Approve or move money; approve contracts; change bank/wallet details; invent business facts; override Policy/Approval/RBAC; modify audit evidence; grant itself or another agent new privileges.

Reporting

Routine system/workflow health → GM/Operations. Domain exceptions → responsible human manager. Material security/financial/business incidents → responsible manager + GM; Owner when severity policy requires.

KPI Families

Routing accuracy; workflow completion; stuck-workflow detection; mean time to detect/escalate; duplicate workflow rate; safe retry success; false escalation rate; unauthorized-action attempts blocked; availability; cost/token per completed workflow.

9. Agent 3 — AI Reservation Service

Mission: Act as the primary professional customer-facing reservation workforce while remaining grounded in verified villa, contract, rate, availability and transaction state.

Runtime Model

One logical service, multiple isolated concurrent workers. Horizontal scaling must be controlled by queue depth, latency, provider limits, token/cost budget and safety limits. Business state must live in durable stores, never only in worker memory.

Capability Workers

  • Inquiry & Requirement Extraction
  • Villa Matching
  • Availability Query
  • Pricing/Rate Calculation Interface
  • Quotation
  • Conversation & Follow-up
  • Tentative/Full Hold Workflow
  • Booking Workflow
  • Amendment/Cancellation Assistance
  • Pre-arrival Communication
  • Human Handoff

Conversation Isolation Minimum

agency_id, conversation_id, customer_id, inquiry_id, channel_id, reservation_id, current_state, requirements, villa_candidates, quote_reference, hold_reference, payment_state, consent_state, human_owner, risk_state.

Detailed Job Description

  • Receive inquiry from approved channels and extract guest requirements.
  • Ask clarification questions only when required information is missing.
  • Search verified inventory and recommend relevant villas.
  • Query availability and preserve source, timestamp and confidence/state semantics.
  • Use deterministic pricing from verified rate/contract versions; do not calculate commercial truth from model memory.
  • Create/send quotations only through approved policy-controlled workflow.
  • Perform professional follow-up without spam or conflicting communication.
  • Request tentative/full hold when policy permits and track expiry.
  • Coordinate booking workflow after applicable gates are satisfied.
  • Communicate payment status but never infer settlement from customer claims/screenshots.
  • Provide verified pre-arrival information and escalate exceptions.

May Autonomously Within Policy

Verified FAQ; requirement extraction; deterministic search/matching; availability query; explain verified rates; generate approved quotation; routine follow-up; alternatives; approved hold request; status communication; verified pre-arrival communication.

Mandatory Escalation

Out-of-policy discount; high-value/complex negotiation; ambiguous/conflicting contract or supplier data; availability conflict; funds after hold expiry; serious complaint; safety/emergency; legal threat; unusual cancellation; sensitive privacy/security matter; customer requests human; AI uncertainty above configured threshold.

Must Not

Invent availability/rates/amenities/policies; claim requested hold is confirmed; claim reported payment is received; fabricate supplier confirmation; override cancellation/commercial terms; expose another customer's data; place raw passport/identity documents into LLM context.

Reporting

Operations/Reservation Manager → GM. Material customer/business exceptions may escalate to GM. Owner receives executive aggregates/material exceptions, not routine chats.

KPI Families

First-response latency; response SLA; inquiry completeness; recommendation relevance; availability/quotation/booking accuracy; inquiry→quote and quote→booking conversion; follow-up completion; confirmation accuracy; duplicate booking incidents; hallucinated business facts; inappropriate autonomous actions; escalation precision; human takeover rate; CSAT; cost per confirmed booking.

11. Agent 5 — Finance Admin AI Agent

Mission: Automate finance administration, matching, reconciliation assistance and financial intelligence while possessing zero authority to approve or move funds.

Workers / Capabilities

  • Client AR Monitor
  • Supplier AP Monitor
  • Payment Matching
  • Reconciliation Assistance
  • Invoice Validation
  • Settlement Calculation
  • Payment Due Monitor
  • Unallocated Receipt Detection
  • Duplicate Invoice/Payment Detection
  • Financial Exception Detection
  • Cash/Exposure Reporting

Detailed Job Description

  • Monitor invoices, aging, due dates and verified payment events.
  • Match receipts to invoices using authoritative evidence and deterministic rules.
  • Identify partial/over/unmatched/duplicate payments and create exceptions.
  • Validate supplier invoice references and calculate settlement proposals.
  • Prepare payment requests and supporting evidence for human review.
  • Produce reconciliation and management reports.
ABSOLUTE DENY: approve/execute payment; approve/execute refund; change/approve bank details; change crypto destination; sign blockchain transaction; access private keys/seed phrases/signing credentials; control treasury wallets; mark unverified funds as received; bypass maker-checker/approval; alter evidence or audit records.

Payment Data Boundary

Raw PAN/card number, CVV/CVC, PIN/sensitive authentication data must not enter the AI context or ordinary application storage. Use hosted/tokenized provider flows. Provider/browser success pages are not authoritative settlement evidence; verified server-side events and reconciliation rules govern payment state.

Reporting

Functional owner: Finance & Accounting Manager. Direct management/exception reporting: General Manager and Owner. Routine reports should be scheduled/configurable; critical exceptions are event-driven.

KPI Families

Reconciliation accuracy; matched-receipt rate; unmatched aging; duplicate-payment prevention; invoice validation; settlement accuracy; exception detection; AR aging; reporting timeliness; unauthorized payment/bank change/raw-card exposure (critical zero-tolerance defects); human correction rate.

16. Critical Business Invariants

  • RELEASE_REQUESTED != RELEASED
  • CLIENT_PAYMENT_REPORTED != CLIENT_FUNDS_RECEIVED
  • AI_EXTRACTED != VERIFIED
  • SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE != NOT_AVAILABLE
  • Supplier route must be explicit for commercial truth.
  • Historical booking/financial snapshots are immutable.
  • Approval becomes invalid when its critical target/fingerprint changes.

  • Core booking operations must remain usable when AI is unavailable.

20. Hermes Implementation Guardrails

  1. Implement the six logical components as bounded capabilities; do not create extra autonomous agents without an approved ADR.
  2. Prefer deterministic code for authorization, state transitions, pricing arithmetic, finance rules, idempotency, validation and invariants.
  3. Use LLMs for language understanding/generation, classification, extraction with verification, summarization, reasoning assistance and recommendations—not as the authority for critical state.
  4. All tools require explicit schemas, least privilege and server-side authorization on every invocation.
  5. Do not expose arbitrary SQL, unrestricted filesystem/shell, unrestricted HTTP fetch, payment credentials or secrets to business agents.
  6. Persist durable workflow state outside LLM/worker memory.
  7. Every customer conversation and tenant context must be isolated.
  8. All critical writes require idempotency and concurrency controls.
  9. All external content is untrusted until validated/sanitized according to its use.
  10. Do not treat a screenshot, email, PDF, customer statement or another AI's statement as verified financial/commercial truth without authoritative verification.
  11. Implement observability for latency, errors, token/cost, tool use, exceptions, safety blocks and human corrections.
  12. Do not optimize for autonomy percentage. Optimize for safe task completion, accuracy, business outcome and controlled escalation.

22. Definition of Done for Each Agent

  • Responsibility and data scope are explicit and non-overlapping.
  • Tool allowlist and denylist are implemented server-side.
  • Authority levels and human-approval points are executable controls, not prompt text only.
  • Source-of-truth mappings are enforced.
  • Concurrency/idempotency/retry/failure behavior is tested.
  • Human handoff is tested.
  • Security/adversarial tests pass.
  • Observability and audit evidence exist.
  • KPI telemetry exists before KPI targets are treated as operational truth.
  • Agent can be degraded/suspended/disabled independently.
  • Core system remains functional if the agent/LLM is unavailable.

23. Architecture Freeze — V1

Locked baseline: V1 contains exactly six logical AI components: (1) AI Control Tower / Orchestrator, (2) Commercial AI Agent, (3) AI Reservation Service, (4) Operations AI Agent, (5) Finance Admin AI Agent, and (6) Revenue & Business Intelligence AI Agent.
